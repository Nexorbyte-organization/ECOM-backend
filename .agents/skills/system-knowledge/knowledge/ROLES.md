# Roles and authorization

- `usher`: own profile, payout methods, applications/referrals/history, event browsing, self check-in (QR, code, or location), accepting/declining direct booking and standby invitations (blocked while suspended for no-shows), and leaving a standby list (complete profile required). Complete-profile gates protect selected application/referral/excuse mutations; check-in is not profile-gated.
- `organizer`: owns company profile/events/staff. Owner-only routes include event creation/edit/close/complete, supervisor assignment, WhatsApp creation, bulk and individual settlement creation, payout retry, cash marking, card management, advance funding checkout, funding-mode changes, early payment release, and organization credit (read only; credit is spent on future funding).
- `organizer_member`, `organizer_supervisor`: company workspace reads (including an event's funding summary, without saved cards) plus decisions (accept, reject, or standby), direct booking and standby invitations, check-in points, staff check-in (present/late only; nobody can mark absent), and reviews. Organization users cannot cancel or delete events, directly or by request; only admins can. providerOwnerId resolves company ownership. Current event queries scope to company, not supervisor assignment.
- `admin`: platform users/events/action requests, authenticated settlement and funding lookup, organization tier overrides and credit adjustments, and an overview of underfunded events and failed card refunds; attendance and payment release need no admin decision. An admin can temporarily act as an active, unblocked organization owner through a scoped session; organization routes then use that owner's permissions, while admin routes require stopping the acting session first.
- Organization owners and staff can list, add, and remove the shared favorite usher list, preview the previous team for a company event, and invite that team to an owned open event. Rebooking requires the organization profile to be complete.
- Organization owners and staff can read analytics for their own organization. Admins can read platform-wide analytics; an admin acting as an organization uses only that organization's analytics until the acting session stops.

Event maps: the organizer owner uploads the map and creates/edits/deletes pins and assignments; organizer staff can read the company map. An usher can fetch a map only while hired and assigned, and receives only their own pin details. Assignment accepts hired ushers only.

Authentication rejects blocked accounts and staff with missing/non-organizer/blocked owners. Staff profile gates use the owner. Route role checks do not replace controller record-ownership checks.
